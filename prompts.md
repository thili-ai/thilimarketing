# Prompts

Each lesson gives its prompts in full. They are collected here so you can copy them. Every one starts with the
date to assume, because Claude otherwise assumes the real date.

Replace nothing except where a prompt says "(paste: ...)". Start a new chat for each run unless the lesson says
to continue in the same one. In lessons 1 to 5 run each prompt twice, on two different models, and compare.

## Lesson 1: Who are our customers?
Attach `customers.csv` and `purchases.csv`. A follow-up asks for personas.

## Lesson 2: Did the Onam campaign work?
Attach `outlets.csv`, `outlet_sales_weekly.csv` and `events_calendar.csv`.

## Lesson 3: Who is about to leave?
Attach `customers.csv` and `purchases.csv`.

## Lesson 4: What are customers saying?
Attach `reviews.csv`. Label the fifty reviews in `fifty-reviews-to-label.csv` yourself first.

## Lesson 5: The campaign decision
Attach `decision-memo-template.md`, and paste your own notes.
